# Code of Conduct

Chat Unleashed is a technical project. Everyone participating in its issues, pull requests, reviews or other project-managed spaces should be able to contribute without harassment.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient and constructive, including when reviewing code or disagreeing about design.
- Critique ideas and implementation details rather than attacking people.
- Give credit, respect attribution and consider how changes affect other users.
- Help keep technical discussions focused, accessible and useful.
- Respect other people's privacy and obtain permission before sharing their content.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, bullying or unwelcome personal attacks.
- Sexualized comments or unwanted attention.
- Publishing someone's private information, credentials, logs or messages without consent.
- Deliberate disruption, trolling or repeated bad-faith participation.
- Retaliation against someone for raising a concern in good faith.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies to project-managed community spaces and behavior performed while representing the project. Maintainers may ask participants to change their behavior, remove inappropriate content, close discussions, restrict participation or block accounts when needed. Responses depend on the circumstances and their impact.

To report conduct concerns, contact the repository maintainer privately through an established private channel. Avoid publishing personal information in an issue. Security vulnerabilities follow the separate [security policy](SECURITY.md). Reports will be handled as discreetly as practical, without promising absolute confidentiality.

Maintainers are also expected to follow this policy.
